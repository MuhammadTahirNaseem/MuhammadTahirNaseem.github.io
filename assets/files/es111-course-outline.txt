ES111: Applied Probability and Statistics
Semester: Spring 2026
Instructor: Dr. Muhammad Tahir Naseem

Textbook:
Probability and Statistics for Engineers and Scientists, 9th edition,
by Ronald E. Walpole, Raymond H. Myers, Sharon L. Myers, and Keying Ye.

Course overview:
This course introduces probability, random variables, probability distributions,
sampling, estimation, hypothesis testing, regression, and statistical reasoning
for science and engineering students.
