Summary

Short CV Summary

Dr. Muhammad Tahir Naseem is an academic physicist affiliated with the Faculty of Basic Sciences at the GIK Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology. His work focuses on quantum thermodynamics, open quantum systems, quantum heat transport, Floquet quantum thermal devices, quantum thermometry, and reservoir engineering.
