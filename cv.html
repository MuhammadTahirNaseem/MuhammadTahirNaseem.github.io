Profile

Academic Profile

Dr. Muhammad Tahir Naseem is Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Basic Sciences at GIK Institute and Principal Investigator of QuTIC Lab. His research focuses on quantum technologies based on photonic and atomic systems, including quantum computing, quantum devices, open quantum systems, quantum optics, quantum thermodynamics, and quantum heat transport.
