Profile

About

Dr. Muhammad Tahir Naseem is affiliated with the Faculty of Basic Sciences at the GIK Institute of Engineering Sciences and Technology. His academic work focuses on theoretical and applied questions in quantum physics, particularly the thermodynamic behavior of open quantum systems.

His research examines how engineered quantum environments, thermal reservoirs, and measurement strategies can be used to understand and design quantum thermal technologies.

Quantum Thermodynamics

Quantum heat engines, refrigerators, thermometers, and heat transport mechanisms.
