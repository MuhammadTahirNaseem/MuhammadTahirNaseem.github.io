Mission

Lab Mission

QuTIC Lab develops theoretical and computational approaches for understanding thermodynamic processes in quantum systems. The group focuses on how reservoirs, measurement, coherence, and external driving influence transport, sensing, and device behavior at microscopic scales.

The lab is led by Dr. Muhammad Tahir Naseem, Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Basic Sciences at GIK Institute. Its broader aim is to connect foundational quantum theory with emerging applications in quantum thermal technologies, photonic and atomic systems, and quantum computing.

Research Themes

Core Research Directions

Quantum Thermodynamics and Heat Transport

Modeling energy flow, thermal currents, and non-equilibrium behavior in quantum systems coupled to reservoirs.

Floquet Quantum Thermal Devices

Studying periodically driven quantum machines, including thermal diodes, heat engines, and tunable heat-flow models.

Quantum Thermometry and Information

Using Fisher information and quantum probes to analyze precision temperature estimation in small environments.
