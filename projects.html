Projects

Group research projects in quantum thermodynamics, devices, and computing.

Selected QuTIC Lab project areas are listed below in an editable card format. Each card summarizes the research goal, methods, current status, related outputs, and keywords.

Status: Active

Floquet Quantum Thermal Diode

A Floquet quantum thermal diode uses periodic driving to control the direction and magnitude of heat flow in a quantum system. The project studies how time-dependent modulation can enhance thermal rectification and produce regimes that are not available in static devices.

Methods Used

Floquet theory, open quantum system modeling, master equations, heat-current calculations, and numerical parameter scans.

Related Outputs / Publications

Related outputs include Quantum optical two-atom thermal diode, Heat rectification by two qubits coupled with Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction, and Quantum thermal rectification via state-dependent two-photon dissipation.

  • Floquet driving
  • Thermal diode
  • Rectification
  • Heat transport

Status: Active

Quantum Thermometry with Non-Gaussian States

This project investigates how non-Gaussian quantum states can improve temperature estimation in small thermal environments. The focus is on identifying probe states and measurement strategies that increase sensitivity beyond standard Gaussian-state approaches.

Methods Used

Quantum Fisher information, parameter estimation theory, non-Gaussian state analysis, open-system dynamics, and numerical optimization.

Related Outputs / Publications

Related outputs include Optimal strategies for transient and equilibrium quantum thermometry using Gaussian and non-Gaussian probes, Mixing thermal coherent states for precision and range enhancement in quantum thermometry, and Low-temperature quantum thermometry boosted by coherence generation.

  • Quantum thermometry
  • Fisher information
  • Non-Gaussian states
  • Quantum metrology

Status: Active

Reservoir-Engineered Mechanical Cat States

Reservoir engineering offers a controlled way to prepare and stabilize nonclassical states through dissipation. This project studies mechanical cat states in hybrid quantum systems, with emphasis on how structured environments can protect coherent superpositions against decoherence.

Methods Used

Lindblad master equations, dissipative state preparation, optomechanical modeling, steady-state analysis, and decoherence diagnostics.

Related Outputs / Publications

Related outputs include Reservoir-engineered mechanical cat states with a driven qubit, Ground-state cooling of mechanical resonators by quantum reservoir engineering, and Engineering entanglement between resonators by hot environment.

  • Reservoir engineering
  • Mechanical cat states
  • Optomechanics
  • Decoherence

Status: In development

Quantum Thermal Transistor Models

Quantum thermal transistors aim to control a heat current through a small change in another thermal or quantum control channel. This project develops model systems that display amplification, switching, and tunable heat-flow behavior at the quantum scale.

Methods Used

Few-level quantum models, master-equation methods, steady-state heat-current analysis, amplification measures, and numerical simulations.

Related Outputs / Publications

Related outputs include the QTD 2021 conference talk Quantum thermal transistor boosted by reservoir engineering and earlier work on quantum heat diodes, heat rectification, and quantum heat managers.

  • Thermal transistor
  • Heat amplification
  • Quantum devices
  • Open systems

Status: Infrastructure and training

Quantum Compiler Research

This project focuses on improving quantum circuits before execution on quantum hardware or simulators. The emphasis is on circuit optimization, transpilation, gate-count reduction, depth reduction, and hardware-aware circuit mapping.

Methods Used

Quantum circuit analysis, transpilation workflows, optimization heuristics, gate decomposition, circuit-depth benchmarking, and simulator-based validation.

Related Outputs / Publications

Related activity includes the PSDP 2024 project Establishment of quantum computing lab at GIK Institute - Year 3 and GIK workshops on quantum gates, quantum algorithms, and quantum machine learning.

  • Quantum compiling
  • Circuit optimization
  • Transpilation
  • Quantum circuits
